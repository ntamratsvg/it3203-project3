Essential IT Concepts

Understanding the foundation of our topic requires a grasp of these core terms:

Concept Term Technical Definition
Scalability The ability of a system to handle a growing amount of work by adding resources.
Virtualization The act of creating a virtual version of something, like hardware platforms or storage.
Latency The delay before a transfer of data begins following an instruction for its transfer.
