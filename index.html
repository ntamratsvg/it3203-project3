Exploring Cloud Computing

What is the Cloud?

Cloud computing is the on-demand delivery of computing power, database storage, applications, and other IT resources via the internet[cite: 2, 4]. Instead of managing physical servers or hard drives, you access these technologies through providers like Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud[cite: 6].

Why It Matters

The cloud is the backbone of the modern mobile web[cite: 3]. It allows your smartphone to access massive amounts of data without draining its own battery or filling up its local storage[cite: 3]. By shifting the processing power to remote data centers, the cloud enables the fast, responsive experiences we expect on our devices today[cite: 3, 8].
