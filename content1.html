Evolution of the Cloud

Before the cloud, businesses had to buy expensive physical servers and keep them in cooled rooms[cite: 2]. In the early 2000s, companies like Amazon and Google realized they could rent out their extra server space to others over the internet, leading to the "Cloud" we use today[cite: 2].

The Three Service Models

  • IaaS (Infrastructure): You rent the raw hardware and manage the software yourself[cite: 1].
  • PaaS (Platform): You focus on writing code while the provider manages the servers[cite: 1].
  • SaaS (Software): You simply use an app over the web, like Google Drive or Netflix[cite: 1].
